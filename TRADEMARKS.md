# Name and mark

The code in this repository is MIT-licensed (see [LICENSE](./LICENSE)). The MIT
license covers the code. It does not cover the project's name or its mark, which
are what tell people a copy of Augur is the real one.

"Augur", as the name of this feedback module, and the eye-in-triangle mark in
[`branding/`](./branding) belong to Krishnachandran Ramachandran (kaezee). They are
not registered trademarks; this page is a statement of how they may be used, so
nobody has to guess.

## Fine, no need to ask

- Saying your product uses Augur: "Feedback by Augur", "Built with Augur", a line
  in your credits or changelog.
- Leaving the shipped "by Augur" byline on, as it comes.
- Writing about Augur, reviewing it, comparing it, teaching with it.
- Linking to this repository with the name or the mark.

## Please don't

- Publish a fork or a derived module under the name Augur, or a name close enough
  to be mistaken for it. Fork freely, then give your version its own name.
- Use the mark as the logo or icon of your own product or fork.
- Imply that the author endorses, maintains or supports your product when
  that isn't the case.

## Unsure?

Open an issue or reach out via [github.com/kaezee](https://github.com/kaezee). The
answer will almost always be yes.
